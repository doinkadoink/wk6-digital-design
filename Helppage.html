☕ Getting Started with Chattea

Welcome to Chattea!

Chattea is a platform for coffee enthusiasts to connect, share experiences, and discover new coffee spots across the Gold Coast.

Creating an Account

To get started:

  1. Click the Sign Up button on the homepage
  2. Enter your email address and create a password
  3. Complete your profile by adding your coffee preferences
  4. Take the coffee personality quiz to find your perfect match

Guest Access

Not ready to commit? Click "view as guest" to browse public lobbies and explore the platform before creating an account.

🏙️ Using Coffee Lobbies

What are Lobbies?

Lobbies are chat rooms organized by location across the Gold Coast where coffee lovers can connect, share recommendations, and plan meetups.

Joining a Lobby

From the homepage:

  1. Scroll down to view available lobbies
  2. Click on any lobby to join (Southport, Broadbeach, Surfers Paradise, etc.)
  3. Once inside, you can view active users and ongoing conversations

Lobby Etiquette

Please keep conversations friendly and coffee-related. Share your favorite cafes, brewing tips, and arrange meetups with fellow coffee enthusiasts.

💬 Chat Features

Sending Messages

To participate in lobby conversations:

  1. Type your message in the input field at the bottom of the chat
  2. Press Enter or click the Send button
  3. Your message will appear with your avatar and a "Just now" timestamp

Using Emojis

Express yourself with emojis! Use your device's emoji keyboard or copy-paste your favorite coffee emojis: ☕ 🍵 🧋 🥤

Viewing Users

The sidebar shows all active users in the lobby along with their coffee preferences.

⚙️ Account Settings

Managing Your Profile

To update your profile information:

  1. Navigate to the Settings page
  2. Click on "Account" to manage your personal information
  3. Update your username, email, or password as needed

Notification Preferences

Control what notifications you receive:

  1. Go to the Settings page
  2. Select "Notifications"
  3. Toggle which notifications you want to receive

Updating Coffee Preferences

To update your coffee preferences:

  1. Go to the Settings page
  2. Click on "Preferences"
  3. Choose "Retake Preference Quiz" to update your coffee match

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my theme?

Click the coffee cup icon in the top right corner of any page to toggle between hot and iced themes.

Can I create my own lobby?

Currently, lobbies are created by administrators. Contact support if you'd like to suggest a new lobby location.

How do I report inappropriate behavior?

Contact support using the form below or reach out to our Discord support team for immediate assistance.

Is my information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. Check our privacy policy for details.

Why can't I access certain features?

Some features are only available to registered users. Sign up for a free account to access all features.
